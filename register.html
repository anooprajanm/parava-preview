Register your team

Registration opens on 1 December 2026 at 2 PM IST. 24 team slots in one mixed division, then a waitlist.

How it works

  1. Pay for 6 playersPay six players’ fees to the club account. The rest of your squad is paid later.
  2. Fill in the formTeam details, a contact person, and your payment proof and reference number.
  3. Join the queueYour team appears under “Awaiting verification” on the 2027 page, in the order teams registered.
  4. Get confirmedWe check your payment and confirm your slot by email. After 24 teams, registrations go to the waitlist.

Fees and rules

Teams based in India

₹2,000 per player

Pay ₹12,000 to register (6 players), by UPI.

Teams based outside India

USD 25 per player

Pay USD 150 to register (6 players), by wire transfer.

  • Mixed division. Every team needs at least 6 men and 6 women.
  • The fee follows the team’s base, not each player’s nationality.
  • Balance for the rest of your squad: Balance due date
  • Waitlist. Waitlisted teams pay the same registration amount. If no slot opens, it is refunded in full.
  • Refunds if you withdraw: Refund policy
